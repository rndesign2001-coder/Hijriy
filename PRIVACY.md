# Hijriy Taqvim — Maxfiylik siyosati / Privacy Policy

*Oxirgi yangilanish: 2026-yil 9-oktabr*

## O'zbekcha

**Hijriy Taqvim | Namoz vaqtlari** (paket: `uz.hijriy.app`) ilovasi foydalanuvchilarning shaxsiy ma'lumotlarini yig'maydi, saqlamaydi va sotmaydi. Ilovada ro'yxatdan o'tish, reklama va statistika (analitika) xizmatlari yo'q.

**Joylashuv.** Namoz vaqtlari va qibla yo'nalishi siz tanlagan hudud yoki telefon GPS'i asosida **faqat telefonning o'zida** hisoblanadi. Ob-havo ko'rsatish uchun joylashuvingizning taxminiy koordinatalari (taxminan 1 km aniqlikkacha yaxlitlangan) [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) xizmatiga so'rov sifatida yuboriladi. Bu koordinatalar biz tomonimizdan saqlanmaydi va hech kimga berilmaydi.

**Internetdan yuklanadigan ma'lumotlar.** Qur'on tilovati audio fayllari [everyayah.com](https://everyayah.com) dan, Qur'on ma'nolarining o'zbekcha tarjimasi [api.alquran.cloud](https://alquran.cloud) dan yuklab olinadi. Bu so'rovlarda shaxsiy ma'lumot yuborilmaydi.

**Telefonda saqlanadigan ma'lumotlar.** Sozlamalar, xatcho'plar, qazo hisoblagichi va tasbeh hisobi faqat telefoningizda saqlanadi va ilova o'chirilganda o'chib ketadi.

**Ruxsatlar.** Joylashuv (hududni aniqlash uchun, ixtiyoriy), bildirishnomalar va aniq vaqtli signal (namoz vaqti eslatmalari uchun), fon rasmi o'rnatish va internet.

**Bolalar.** Ilova bolalardan ataylab hech qanday ma'lumot yig'maydi.

## English

**Hijriy Taqvim | Namoz vaqtlari** (package `uz.hijriy.app`) does not collect, store or sell personal data. There is no sign-in, no advertising and no analytics.

- Prayer times and qibla direction are calculated **on the device** from the region you choose or the phone's GPS.
- To show the weather, approximate coordinates (rounded to about 1 km) are sent to [Open-Meteo](https://open-meteo.com) as a request. We do not store or share them.
- Quran recitation audio is downloaded from everyayah.com and the Uzbek translation from api.alquran.cloud; no personal data is sent.
- Settings, bookmarks and counters are stored only on your device.
- Permissions: location (optional, to detect your region), notifications and exact alarms (prayer reminders), wallpaper and internet.
